## How to cite standards work

1. Link to the official organization record, not a search result, news repost, or unrelated IEEE document number.
2. Record the item type, release, version, and verification date.
3. Distinguish normative requirements from informative studies and reports.
4. Avoid claiming that a research paper is standards-compliant unless the cited standard and conformance evidence establish it.
5. Recheck status before relying on this page, because drafts and releases can change after 2026-07-18.

## Deliberately excluded claims

This snapshot does not infer ISAC-specific spectrum allocation from a general WRC decision, does not turn a study report into a specification, and does not assign unverified KPI or use-case counts to 3GPP documents.
